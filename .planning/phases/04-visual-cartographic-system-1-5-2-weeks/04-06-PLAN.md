<objective>
Derive the interior-border mesh at build time and bind it to a check that can actually fail.

Purpose: `04-09` renders country borders as their **own** shared line layer so coastlines can go to
`none` and the map reads like the owner's Eurostat reference — thin dark lines *between* countries,
nothing around the coasts. That layer needs a geometry artifact, and the artifact needs a gate that
fails on drift, or the mesh silently diverges from the polygons the first time either changes.

`04-RESEARCH.md § Interior-Border Mesh` measured the derivation this session: `mapshaper 0.7.48` is
already a devDependency, `-innerlines` does exactly the job in **0.22 s**, and two runs produced an
**identical SHA-256**. It emits a `GeometryCollection` (not a `FeatureCollection`) of **327
`LineString`s** over **19,624** coordinate points, **366,767 bytes** at `precision=0.0001` — about
16 % of the polygon asset's 2,295,448 bytes, needing no simplification.

Output: the mesh artifact, a manifest record, and a derivational `--check` branch RED-proved twice.
</objective>

<the_trap_this_plan_must_avoid>
**Do not bind the mesh to the polygon file's hash.** `ROADMAP.md 04-04` says *"hash-recorded in
`world-manifest.json` beside the polygon asset"*, which reads as if the two hashes should be tied.
They must not be, and the reason is **measured**: `04-03` changed `world-modern.geojson`'s bytes (the
twelve units' `isSelectable` / `colorOwnerId`) but the mesh is **insensitive to feature properties** —
`04-RESEARCH.md` re-derived it after flipping `KOS.isSelectable` and got the **same SHA-256**. A
polygon-hash binding would therefore report drift that has not happened, every time a policy field
moves.

The correct shape, and research's ranked recommendation: **bind the mesh to its own SHA-256 and
re-derive it in `--check`.** That is the same discipline `prepareWorldData.mjs` already applies to the
polygon asset (`canonicalBytes.equals(committedBytes)` against a deterministically re-derived buffer,
not a hash lookup), and it fails on **both** a drifted polygon asset and a tampered mesh. The
alternative of a geometry-only cross-digest is correct but adds a bespoke canonicalisation nobody
else in the repo maintains — **rejected.**
</the_trap_this_plan_must_avoid>
