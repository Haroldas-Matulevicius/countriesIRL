<objective>
Render the interior-border mesh as its own layer, and give hover and selection a carrier that does not
depend on a coastline stroke that no longer exists.

Purpose: `04-08` made coastlines quiet. That is only half the reference look — the Eurostat image has
**thin dark lines clearly present between countries** and effectively nothing around the coasts. This
plan draws those lines from `04-06`'s derived mesh.

It also closes the interaction question `04-CONTEXT.md` left open: *"if a coastal country's outline
goes to `none`, hover and selection feedback for that country needs another carrier. Unresolved; name
it in `04-05`."* `04-UI-SPEC.md § 6.9` names it, and this plan builds it.

Output: two new camera-inside layers, a date-line-wrapped mesh, an export-invisible highlight layer,
a two-direction PNG border gate, and the ROADMAP amendment CD-11 requires.
</objective>

  weight states are *"re-expressed on it [the interior mesh]"*. **That is not achievable.** The mesh is
  a shared line layer: each segment belongs to **two** countries, so weighting a segment would
  highlight both. `04-UI-SPEC.md § 6.9` specifies a dedicated `data-editor-only` highlight layer
  instead, which the export clone already strips wholesale — so it **provably cannot move exported
  pixels**. **Task 3 amends the roadmap line.** Reported, not silently diverged from.

  keeping in the plan: interaction state **must never reach the PNG anyway** (the sanitizer already

  that structural instead of incidental); it **decouples** the creator's coastline choice from feedback
  legibility, so choosing `none` no longer costs the creator their hover affordance; and it is
  **RED-provable on its own subject** — select, export, assert no selection stroke, then delete the
  `data-editor-only` attribute and watch the assertion go red.

  it a creator framed at zoom 8 downloads 8x-thick borders, the exact defect `coding-rules/export.md`
  records.

every polygon; the mesh needs the same wrapping, or a Pacific-framed composition shows fills with no
interior borders on the wrapped copies. `04-RESEARCH.md § Open Questions` item 6 flags this as not
named in the roadmap. Reuse the existing offset mechanism rather than authoring a second one.

Take the stroke colour from `settings.borderColor` and the width from `STROKE_WEIGHT_UNITS` keyed by
`settings.interiorWeight` — the **same table** `04-08` gave the country paths and the export clone, so
the editor and the download cannot disagree about what `medium` means. Both as inline attributes.

Any new CSS goes in `src/styles/MapCanvas.css`; measure the selector inventory before and after and
move the ceiling with a stated reason in the same commit.
