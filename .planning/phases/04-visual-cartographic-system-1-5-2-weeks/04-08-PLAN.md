<objective>
Make the export path stop overwriting the creator's border choices, and give an uncoloured country a
visible fill.

🚨 **This plan exists because of a measured blocking dependency, and it must be in the plan rather
than discovered in execution.** `sanitizeExportClone` currently hard-sets `stroke: #000000` and
`stroke-width: 0.75` on **every** `path.scene-path, path.country-path` in the export clone — as an
attribute **and** as an inline style (`src/utils/export.ts` ~:334-356). Read literally: **the exporter
re-paints a black 0.75 stroke onto every country path regardless of what the editor rendered.** So
D4-08's "coastlines at `none`" and the ROADMAP's *"outlines all but disappear against water"* are
**impossible in the PNG until this function changes** — the editor would show unstroked coasts and the
download would ship black ones, the exact quieter-failure class `coding-rules/export.md` warns about.

**This is not a bug in the current code.** The normalisation exists because wrapped date-line repeats
must not ship a 2px selection border, and the `non-scaling-stroke` pin beside it is the recorded fix
for borders that "looked super thick in the download only". **Replace it. Never delete it.**

D4-09 rides along because it is what makes near-invisible coastlines survivable: with white water and
unstroked coasts, a white country would vanish. An uncoloured country renders flat grey, and the fill
is creator-changeable.

Output: a named stroke-weight vocabulary, a replaced export normaliser, two new `Map style` sections,
and a coastline PNG gate that has been seen to fail in both directions.
</objective>
