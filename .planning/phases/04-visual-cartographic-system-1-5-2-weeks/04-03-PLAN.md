<objective>
Reverse the neutral-unit colour policy: **all twelve `colorPolicy: "neutral"` units become colorable.**

Owner decision, stated directly in `04-CONTEXT.md` D4-10: *"I want kosovo and the othe regions
colorable, there should not be a region that is not colorable."*

Purpose: this is **the largest hidden cost in the phase and it is not a data edit.**
`04-RESEARCH.md § D4-10: Measured Blast Radius` enumerates **twelve touch points**, including three
separate `throw`s in `scripts/prepareWorldData.mjs` that encode the policy being reversed, three
count sites, a `SceneFeature` union variant under which a colourable neutral unit **does not
typecheck**, and `LOGICAL_CORE_COUNT` in the e2e harness, which **every e2e spec inherits via
`waitForApp`**. Sequencing it in wave 2 means the suite settles at 207 before nine later plans build
e2e gates on top of it.

There is also a product consequence Phase 4 depends on: with no "not colorable" bucket left,
`NEUTRAL_UNIT_COLOR` (`#E5E7EB`) is **freed** to mean simply *uncoloured* (D4-09, implemented in
`04-08`). The grey-vs-grey ambiguity raised during discussion dissolves rather than needing a second grey.

Output: an amended manifest and world asset, a `--check` that reports 207 and can still refuse a
drifted asset, a runtime that admits the twelve, and four documents brought back into agreement.
</objective>
