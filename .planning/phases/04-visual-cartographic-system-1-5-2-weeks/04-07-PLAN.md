<carried_contract_disagreements>
- **CD-1 — D4-05 (360px) vs the approved `03-UI-SPEC.md`, which says `280` in eleven places.** The
  approved spec **outranks `Design.md`** and two of its 280-keyed rules become **obsolete** while one
  is **relaxed with a corrected reason**. `04-UI-SPEC.md § 1` holds a 13-row disposition table which
  **is** the required annotation. **Task 1 lands it in `03-UI-SPEC.md` and `Design.md:257,271` in the
  same commit as the width.** Reported, never silently absorbed.
- **The non-obvious half, stated so a careless renumber cannot undo it.** The near-size adjacency rule
  and the `--text-subheading` ban read as if they are about column width, but their actual subject is
  **a 2px size difference reading as an accident rather than a hierarchy** — just as illegible at
  360px as at 280px. **Both stay in force**, re-reasoned as "within any tool panel". A renumber that
  treats them as satisfied by the widening reintroduces 16px sub-headings into the panel, which is
  exactly the drift the annotation exists to prevent.
- **What the widening actually buys, measured.** At 360 minus 2 x 16 = **328px** of content, two
  full-phrase controls on one row still do **not** fit: 24 characters at `--text-body-sm` with Inter's
  1.0202em worst-case advance is 342.8px of glyphs alone, plus an 8px gap and two 16px paddings is
  ~382.8px against 328px available. So `03-UI-SPEC.md:718`/`:798`'s **practice is preserved** and only
  their **stated reason** is corrected. Icon-only rows, single-word pill groups, and a label plus one
  short control are the intended use of the extra 80px. Recorded so nobody later "discovers" the width
  and puts `Reset All Colors` beside `Apply Color`.
</carried_contract_disagreements>
