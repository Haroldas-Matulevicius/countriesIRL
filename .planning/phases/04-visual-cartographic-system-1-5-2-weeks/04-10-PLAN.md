<objective>
Add the title and footer gradient bands: two edge-anchored rects that fade from the current surface
colour to transparent, giving overlaid type something to sit on.

Purpose: the owner's Eurostat reference is **inset with white margins and the title above the map**.
CountriesIRL has been **full-bleed since Phase 3** — the map reaches every edge — so a title must
*overlay* the map. **That is precisely the job the band exists to do.** `04-CONTEXT.md § specifics`
names this as the one structural difference the planner must not gloss.

D4-16 is specific about the colour: the band fades from the **current surface colour**, not from
hardcoded white. On white water it is invisible and harmless — the reference has no visible bands, and
that is correct behaviour. On a tinted surface it still separates title from map. The band colour is
therefore **derived state that must serialize into the export subtree**, because a serialised SVG
rasterised as an image sees no host stylesheet.

Output: a pure clamp module, two serialized gradient layers, editor-only drag handles with keyboard
equivalents, and a PNG gate that fails on removal **and** on inversion.
</objective>
