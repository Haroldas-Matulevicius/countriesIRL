<objective>
Delete the legend's box chrome outright, and move its default position to where the owner's reference
puts it.

**Owner verbatim, about the reference:** *"the legend on the left edge, it needs to be that subtle.
That level of clean. It can't be like a big ass box. Needs to be well strutured out."* The reference's
legend has **no container at all** — no background panel, no border, no fill opacity. It sits directly
on the map surface. That is the direct source of D4-11.

persisted fields come out of `LegendState`, which is **one-way** — undoing it needs a storage
migration — and which cascades into normalisers, validators, an editor fieldset, a component's colour
table, a `Design.md` exemption, a live invariant, and a requirement.

bit too high"*): today's default is `top-left` at `x = y = 32`, which is **3% from the top of the
square** and directly under where the title band now goes.

Output: a chrome-free legend, a band-aware inset, and four documents amended rather than left false.
</objective>
