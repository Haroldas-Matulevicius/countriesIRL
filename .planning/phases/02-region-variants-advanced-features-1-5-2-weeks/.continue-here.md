## Live invariants — do not regress

1. **Selection/color can never reach a country absent from the active scene.** Eleven routes
   were enumerated and gated. `commitScene` reconciles selection in the same write as the
   snapshot switch; `ColorPicker.selectableCountryIds` is required and intersected. Undo/redo
   is safe only because history stores **colors only, never selection** — if you ever put
   selection into a history snapshot, you reopen this.
2. **Nothing reads `legend.position` raw on a render or export path.** Everything goes
   through `resolveLegendPosition`/`resolveLegendRender` in `src/utils/legend.ts`. One bypass
   reintroduces legend content being clipped out of the exported PNG.
3. **One `MapCanvasHandle`, one `svg.map-canvas`** across the 1200px transition. Guarded by
   `data-camera-owner-sentinel`.
4. **CountryList and Locate keep the unfiltered modern 195-core catalog.** Out-of-scene rows
   are *disabled*, never removed.
5. **The period selector is catalog-driven.** Deferred snapshots must stay structurally
   unreachable, and a manifest label can never override an approved catalog label.
6. **Export strips semantics, never geometry.** Every visible wrapped date-line path stays in
   the clone; roles, titles, ids, tab stops, all `aria-*`, editor state, and the outgoing
   crossfade layer are removed. Border normalization targets `path.scene-path` — a
   `path.country-path`-only rule silently leaves the 2px selection border on decorative
   wrapped repeats. A legend rendered outside the canonical SVG is a hard
   `invalid-composition` failure, never a silently legend-less PNG.
7. **Legend opacity is a single 0-100 scale.** A stored 0-1 fraction is repaired to percent
   and reported, not silently clamped.
