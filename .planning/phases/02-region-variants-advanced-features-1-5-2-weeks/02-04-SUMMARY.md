## Decisions Made

- The selectable set is fixed to the reviewed 195-state core; no source `TYPE`, sovereign field, or display-name inference is allowed at runtime.
- Clear dependencies inherit through explicit core IDs. Cook Islands, Niue, Gibraltar, and disputed/indeterminate units remain neutral and non-selectable.
- The generator preserves exact upstream geometry because the hybrid merge needs no named topology operation; mapshaper remains reserved for future explicit dissolve/clip/erase/simplify work.
- Core logical IDs may differ from Natural Earth source IDs, with `SSD`/`SDS` and `PSE`/`PSX` handled through explicit joins.
